Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ENT, surgeries, and treatments:

What is the recovery period after rhinoplasty?

Most swelling and bruising subside within 2 to 3 weeks, but the final shape of the nose continues to refine over 6 to 12 months.

Are surgeries for snoring and sleep apnea effective?

Yes, identifying the exact site of airway obstruction and treating it greatly improves breathing and sleep quality.

When should I consult an ENT specialist for chronic sinusitis?

If symptoms persist for more than 12 weeks despite standard medical treatments.

Is endoscopic sinus surgery painful?

The procedure is done under general anesthesia, and post-operative discomfort is usually mild and easily managed with prescribed painkillers.
